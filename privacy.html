Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

This notice explains, in plain language, what NOTORITE LLC collects through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you send a quote request, we receive the details you type into the form:

  • Your name and company name
  • Your business email address and, if you add it, your phone number
  • Your business type and the product categories you are interested in
  • Any quantities, budget or message you include

The site does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not ask you to create an account.

How we use it

We use these details only to reply to your request, prepare pricing and terms, and follow up about that request. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for their marketing.

Form submissions may be delivered through an email or form service provider that processes the data on our behalf so it can reach us.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests for as long as needed to respond and to maintain normal business records, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent. Email us at .Reply to any email you received from us and we will handle it.
